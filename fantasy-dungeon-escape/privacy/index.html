Fantasy Dungeon Escape – Privacy Policy

Marshforge Games · Effective 12 November 2026

Fantasy Dungeon Escape (“the game”) is made by Marshforge Games, a solo developer. This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Play data (analytics): events such as stage started, cleared or failed, moves, stars, hints used, ads watched, Endless floor reached, Daily Dungeon played, and session start. They are linked to a random player ID, not to your name or email. We use Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies) to collect them.
  • Leaderboards: your scores and the name shown with them. Leaderboards use Apple Game Center, so your Game Center nickname is shown. Apple’s privacy policy applies to Game Center. Scores already posted to Game Center are kept by Apple and are not removed by Delete my data.
  • Purchases: gold packs are bought through Apple. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made.
  • Ads: the game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. Ads are served by Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners, which may collect device information to show and measure ads. If the game asks for permission to track, ads are personalised only if you allow it on your device.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not sell your data.

Why we use it

To run the game and its leaderboards, to understand which stages are too hard or too easy, to fix problems, and to show the rewarded ads you choose to watch.

Your choices

  • Share play data: turn it off in Settings > Privacy to stop analytics.
  • Delete my data: in Settings > Privacy. This deletes your online player data and play data (analytics) and resets the game’s progress on your device. It cannot be undone.
  • You can also email us to ask for deletion or for a copy of your data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
