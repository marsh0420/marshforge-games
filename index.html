Privacy Policy

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email marshforgegames@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. I usually reply within a few days.

I bought gold but did not receive it

Close and reopen the game while online; the purchase is delivered again. If it still does not arrive, email me with the date of the purchase (the receipt from Apple helps).

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

In the game, open Settings > Privacy > Delete my data, or email me.

I am stuck on a stage

Use a Hint: it shows the next best move. You get free hints every day, and more by watching an optional ad.
